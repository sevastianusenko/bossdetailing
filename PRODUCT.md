## Users

Primary: vehicle owners in the Vancouver WA / Portland OR metro who value their time more than they value a Saturday morning at a wash bay. Two recurring situations:

1. **The busy owner at home or at work.** Books during a weekday, wants the car handled in the driveway or office lot while they keep working. Decides on a phone, often between meetings.
2. **The enthusiast / premium owner.** Drives a car they care about (performance, luxury, a well-kept truck or SUV). Shopping for paint correction or ceramic coating, and is evaluating whether the operator actually knows the craft — process, products, and finish matter more than price.

Secondary (confirmed by the brief's service list, not yet a validated segment): sellers doing pre-sale prep, and small commercial fleet owners in Clark / Multnomah / Washington / Clackamas counties.

## Product Purpose

Boss Auto Detailing LLC brings a full professional detailing bay to the customer's address. Success is a booked on-site appointment: the visitor understands what level of work is being offered, believes this operator is a craftsman rather than a mobile wash, and either calls +1 (509) 224-8299 or submits the request form.

## Positioning

Fully self-contained mobile detailing at a shop-level standard. The van carries its own water, power, extraction, steam and polishing equipment, so work that normally requires a fixed bay — multi-stage paint correction, ceramic coating, hot-water carpet extraction, leather care — happens in the customer's driveway. The competing claims a neighbor could not truthfully copy: shop-grade correction and coating performed on-site, and one operator covering both sides of the Columbia (WA and OR) as a single service area.

## Product Principles

1. **Convenience is the hook; craft is the close.** The offer must land in one line ("we bring the shop to your driveway"), but the reason to choose this operator over the cheapest mobile wash is visible standard of work.
2. **The phone is the primary device and the primary conversion.** Calling must never be more than a thumb away, and the form must be completable one-handed.
3. **Geography is a product feature.** Two states, one operator: the service area is a selling point and the main organic-search surface, not a footer afterthought.
4. **Scope honesty.** Price ranges, vehicle sizes and what each tier includes are stated plainly; a visitor who cannot self-qualify will not call.
5. **Never fabricate proof.** No invented reviews, counts, awards, or certifications — placeholders instead, flagged for the client.
